PART 1: APPLICANT JOURNEY (FROM ACCOUNT CREATION TO INTERVIEW SCHEDULE)
================================================================================

--- STEP 1: REGISTRATION & SECURITY ---
- Open registration page (https://elitecruit.onrender.com/register)
- Select "Applicant" role and fill in name, email, and password
- Submit registration and verify success message
- Receive 6-digit 2FA verification code in email inbox (or check Spam folder)
- Enter 6-digit code on the 2FA verification screen
- Verify successful login redirect to applicant dashboard
- Test 45-second resend cooldown timer
- Accept Terms & Conditions prompt on initial login

--- STEP 2: PROFILE & RESUME SETUP ---
- Complete Applicant Profile (Contact info, headline, location, bio)
- Test ATS Converter (Upload resume file to parse and extract data)
- Test Document to System conversion (Verify parsed text populates profile)
- Upload certificates and credentials under "Categories ng certification"
- Test document upload file limits (Ensure files under 10MB upload cleanly)
- Verify uploaded documents can be viewed, downloaded, or deleted

--- STEP 3: JOB SEARCH & DISCOVERY ---
- Test standard Job Search with keyword search and filters
- Test Geographing (Job location mapping and nearby distance filters)
- Test Swiping Job Search (Interactive card swiping discovery)
- Test fresh graduate tags filtering (Filter jobs open to fresh grads)
- View full Job Details page (Responsibilities, qualifications, company info)

--- STEP 4: JOB APPLICATION & TRACKING ---
- Click "Apply Now" on a selected job post
- Attach resume/cover letter and submit application
- Verify confirmation message appears after submission
- Try applying again to verify duplicate application prevention works
- Open "My Applications" tab to verify application status displays "Pending"
- Check in-app notification bell for application submission alert

--- STEP 5: INTERVIEW & STATUS UPDATES ---
- Check status updates when employer changes status (Under Review / Shortlisted)
- Receive notification when an employer schedules an interview
- Open "Interviews" section to view scheduled interview details
- Verify interview date, start time, end time, and stage display properly
- Verify meeting link or on-site address is clickable and accurate
- Test "Accept Interview" or "Decline Interview" response buttons
- Verify response updates properly in applicant schedule view

PART 2: EMPLOYER JOURNEY (FROM ACCOUNT CREATION TO CREATING SCHEDULE)
================================================================================

--- STEP 1: REGISTRATION & COMPANY ONBOARDING ---
- Open registration page (https://elitecruit.onrender.com/register)
- Select "Employer" role and fill in company name, recruiter email, and password
- Submit registration and receive 6-digit 2FA code via email
- Enter 2FA code and verify successful login to employer dashboard
- Complete Company Profile (Company name, logo, industry, website, description)
- Verify company profile displays correctly in public preview

--- STEP 2: JOB POSTING & MANAGEMENT ---
- Click "Post a Job" to open the job creation form
- Fill in Job Title, Department, Job Type, Salary, and Description
- Verify "Location compressed" display works (Clean city/province badge)
- Add "Fresh graduate tags" to test entry-level badge visibility
- Publish the job post and verify it appears in the active job listings
- Test editing an existing job post
- Test closing and reopening a job post

--- STEP 3: CANDIDATE SOURCING & REVIEW ---
- Open "Applicant Reviewer" tab for an active job post
- Test Drop down job applicant reviewer (Compact dropdown candidate view)
- View applicant details (Resume, parsed skills, uploaded certificates)
- Move applicant through pipeline stages (Pending -> Under Review -> Shortlisted)
- Rate applicant using Candidate Ratings (1 to 5 stars + feedback comment)
- Test Direct Job Invitations (Invite qualified candidates from talent pool)

--- STEP 4: INTERVIEW SCHEDULING ---
- Click "Schedule Interview" for a shortlisted applicant
- Enter Interview Title (e.g. "Technical Interview - Round 1")
- Select Interview Stage (Initial Screening, Technical, Final HR, On-Site)
- Select Interview Type (Online / Video Call or On-Site)
- Enter Meeting Link (Zoom/Google Meet) or Physical Address
- Select Interview Date, Start Time, and End Time
- Click "Confirm & Schedule Interview"
- Verify interview appears in employer "Scheduled Interviews" calendar/list
- Verify automated notification is dispatched to applicant
